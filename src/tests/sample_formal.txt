The committee convened on Tuesday to examine the proposed amendment.
Members reviewed the financial implications of each clause, and the
chair invited written comments from the legal advisor.

Moreover, the report identified three risks that warrant further
inquiry. Therefore, the secretariat shall circulate a revised draft
before the next session, accompanied by supporting evidence.

Notwithstanding the agreed timetable, the rapporteur requested an
extension of two weeks. The committee accepted the request and adjourned.
